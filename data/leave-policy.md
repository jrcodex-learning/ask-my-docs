# Leave policy

Everyone at Brightpath Studio gets 24 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. New
employees who join partway through the year get a share of that, worked out by month: two
days for every full month left in the year after their start date.

## Booking leave

Book leave in the HR portal at least two weeks before you want to take it. Leave of five
days or more in a row needs your team lead's approval as well as the HR portal booking.

## Carrying days over

You can carry up to five unused days into the next year. Carried days must be used by the
end of March, or they are lost. Unused days beyond five are not paid out.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Tell your team lead before 10:00 on the first
day you are ill. For more than three days in a row, upload a doctor's note to the HR portal.

## Parental leave

Birth parents get 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Other parents get 6 weeks of paid
parental leave, which can be taken any time in the first year after the child arrives.
