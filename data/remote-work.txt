Brightpath Studio is a hybrid company. Every team works together in the Pune studio on Tuesdays and Thursdays. On the other three weekdays you may work from home, or from anywhere in India with a reliable internet connection.

The Pune studio is open on weekdays from 08:00 to 20:00. It is closed at weekends and on public holidays.

You may work from another country for up to 30 days a year. Ask HR at least a month before you go, because some countries have tax or visa rules even for short stays.

The company pays up to 15,000 rupees, once, towards a desk, a chair or a monitor for working from home. Keep the receipt and claim it like any other expense.

Wherever you work, be online and reachable between 11:00 and 16:00 India time. These are the core hours. Outside them, plan your day however suits you.
