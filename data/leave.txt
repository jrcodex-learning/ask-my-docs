Everyone at Brightpath Studio gets 24 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. If you join partway through the year, you get two days of annual leave for every full month left in the year.

To book annual leave, use the HR portal at least two weeks before the first day you want off. Leave of five days or more in a row also needs your team lead's approval.

You can carry up to five unused days of annual leave into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by the end of March, or they are lost. Unused days are never paid out as money.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. If you are ill, tell your team lead before 10:00 on your first sick day. If you are ill for more than three days in a row, upload a doctor's note to the HR portal.

Birth parents get 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Other parents get 6 weeks of paid parental leave, which they can take any time in the first year after the child arrives.
