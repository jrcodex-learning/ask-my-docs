# Expenses

Claim work expenses in the finance app within 60 days of paying. Claims older than 60 days
are not paid. Every claim needs a photo of the receipt; a bank statement on its own is not
enough.

## Travel

Book trains and flights through the travel desk, not yourself, so the company pays directly.
Economy class only for flights under six hours. For a taxi to the airport or a client, keep
the app receipt.

## Meals

When travelling for work, the company covers meals up to 1,500 rupees a day. Alcohol is never
covered. Team lunches need the team lead's approval before the lunch, not after.

## Learning budget

Each person has a learning budget of 40,000 rupees a year for books, courses and conference
tickets. It does not roll over. Ask your team lead before spending more than 10,000 rupees
at once.

## Payment

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.
