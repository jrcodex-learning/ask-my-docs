# Remote work

Brightpath Studio is a hybrid company. The studio in Pune is open on weekdays from 08:00 to
20:00, and every team spends Tuesday and Thursday in the studio together. The other three
days you may work from home or anywhere else in India with a reliable connection.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to 30 days a year. Ask HR at least a month ahead,
because some countries have tax or visa rules that apply even to short stays.

## Equipment for home

The company pays up to 15,000 rupees, once, for a desk chair, a desk or a monitor for your
home setup. Keep the receipt and claim it like any other expense.

## Core hours

Wherever you work, be reachable between 11:00 and 16:00 India time. Outside those core
hours, plan your day however suits you, as long as your work gets done.
