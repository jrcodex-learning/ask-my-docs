# Your first week

Welcome to Brightpath Studio! This page explains what happens in your first five days.

## Day one

Arrive at the Pune studio at 10:00. Your buddy, a teammate picked to help you settle in, meets
you at reception, gives you your laptop and access card, and has lunch with you.

## Accounts

IT sets up your email, chat and code-hosting accounts before you arrive. On day one you set a
password and turn on two-factor sign-in for each of them (see the IT security page).

## Meetings

On day two you meet your team lead for an hour to agree on goals for your first 90 days. On
day three you join the Wednesday all-hands at 17:00, where new people say hello.

## Your first task

By the end of the week you ship one small change, with your buddy reviewing it. Small is the
point: it teaches you how work flows here.

## Probation

Probation lasts three months. Halfway through, you and your team lead have a check-in.
