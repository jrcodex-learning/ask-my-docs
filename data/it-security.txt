Use the company password manager for every work account. Work passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Never use a work password for anything outside work.

Turn on two-step sign-in for your email, chat, code account and the HR portal. Use the authenticator app rather than text messages whenever you can.

Lock your laptop screen whenever you walk away from it, even in the studio. Install updates within seven days of being asked.

Never click links in emails that ask you to sign in or pay urgently. Forward them to the security team and delete them. Nobody at Brightpath will ever ask for your password.

Client files stay in the company drive. Never copy them to a personal laptop, a USB stick or a personal cloud account.
