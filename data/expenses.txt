Claim work expenses in the finance app within 60 days of paying. Claims older than 60 days are not paid. Every claim needs a photo of the receipt; a bank statement alone is not enough.

Book work trains and flights through the travel desk, so the company pays directly. Fly economy class for flights under six hours. For a taxi to the airport or to a client, keep the taxi app's receipt and claim it.

When you travel for work, the company pays for meals up to 1,500 rupees a day. Alcohol is never paid for. A team lunch needs your team lead's approval before the lunch, not after.

Each person has a learning budget of 40,000 rupees a year for books, online courses and conference tickets. Unspent budget does not roll over to the next year. Ask your team lead before spending more than 10,000 rupees at once.

Approved expense claims are paid together with your next monthly salary.
