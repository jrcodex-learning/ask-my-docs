On your first day, arrive at the Pune studio at 10:00. Your buddy, a teammate chosen to help you settle in, meets you at reception, gives you your laptop and access card, and has lunch with you.

IT creates your email, chat and code accounts before you arrive. On your first day you choose a password for each account and turn on two-step sign-in.

On day two you meet your team lead for an hour to agree on goals for your first 90 days. On day three you join the Wednesday all-hands meeting at 17:00, where new people introduce themselves.

By the end of your first week you finish one small task, with your buddy reviewing it. Keeping it small is the point: it shows you how work flows here.

Your probation period lasts three months. Halfway through, you and your team lead meet to talk about how it is going.
