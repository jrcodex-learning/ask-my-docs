# IT security

These rules keep the company's and our clients' data safe. They apply to everyone.

## Passwords

Use the company password manager for every work account. Passwords must be at least 14
characters long. Never reuse a work password anywhere else.

## Two-factor sign-in

Turn on two-factor sign-in for email, chat, code hosting and the HR portal. Use the
authenticator app, not text messages, whenever a service offers it.

## Laptops

Lock your screen whenever you leave your laptop, even in the studio. Install updates within
seven days of being asked. Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT within one hour, by phone,
on +91 20 5555 0199 (the IT desk's emergency line).

## Suspicious emails

Don't click links in emails that ask you to sign in or pay urgently. Forward them to the
security inbox and delete them. Nobody at Brightpath will ever ask for your password.

## Client data

Client files stay in the company drive. Never copy them to a personal laptop, a USB stick or
a personal cloud account.
